# Built-in Reference Proteins

## Insulin A-chain
Sequence GIVEQCCTSICSLYQLENYCN, 21 residues. Hormone that regulates blood glucose. Reference values: dominant structure alpha helix, instability index 28.5, hydrophobic ratio 42 percent, pI 5.4, minimum model energy -18.2. Healthy and stable, risk score 0.

## Amyloid-beta 1-42 (Abeta42)
Sequence DAEFRHDSGYEVHHQKLVFFAEDVGSNKGAIIGLMVGGVVIA, 42 residues. Cleaved from the amyloid precursor protein. The monomer is a random coil with a role in synaptic regulation, but in Alzheimer's disease it misfolds into beta-sheet-rich fibrils that form plaques in the brain. The app gives it risk score 92 (High). A well-known mutation is E22K.

## GFP fragment
Sequence MSKGEELFTGVVPILVELDGDVNGHKFSVSGEGEGDATYGKLT. Part of green fluorescent protein, a stable beta-barrel used as a fluorescent reporter in labs. Healthy, dominant beta sheet, instability index 31.2.

## HIV-1 protease
Sequence PQITLWQRPLVTIKIGGQLKEALLDTGADDTVLEEMSLPGRWKPK. A viral enzyme that cuts viral polyproteins into functional pieces, so it is a major drug target (protease inhibitors). Dominant beta sheet, high pI 9.2. The app gives it risk score 85 (High) because of its role in HIV/AIDS, not because it is unstable.

## Alpha helix demo
Sequence AELMAELMAELMAELM. A synthetic repeat of strong helix formers (A, E, L, M). It is used to demonstrate a clean alpha-helix prediction and is structurally stable.
